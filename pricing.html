Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan at any time?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any billing differences.
Is there a free trial?
Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial for all plans. No credit card required to get started.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
You can export all your data at any time. After cancellation, your data is securely stored for 30 days before being permanently deleted.
Do you offer discounts for annual billing?
Yes! Save 20% when you choose annual billing. You can switch to annual billing from your account settings at any time.
