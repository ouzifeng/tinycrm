We believe that powerful software shouldn't be complicated. That's why we built TinyCRM to be the simplest, most intuitive CRM you'll ever use.

Our Mission

We started TinyCRM because we were frustrated with the complexity of existing CRM solutions. Too many features, confusing interfaces, and steep learning curves were preventing teams from actually using their CRM effectively.

Our mission is to create software that gets out of your way and lets you focus on what matters most: building relationships with your customers and growing your business.
